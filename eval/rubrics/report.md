# Report human rubric (1-5)

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relevance | off-topic | partly relevant | precise to topic |
| Readability | hard to follow | readable | clear structure |
| Trust | no citations | partial citations | every claim traceable |
| Actionability | useless | generic | tells what to read/try next |

Two reviewers score independently; report mean and disagreement.
